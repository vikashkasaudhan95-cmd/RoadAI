Report lifecycle

RoadAI's proposed workflow

01 / REPORT

Capture

Category, location, time, optional evidence.

02 / VERIFY

Review

Check freshness, duplicates and corroboration.

03 / ALERT

Inform safely

Future proximity alerts should avoid distracting drivers.

04 / RESOLVE

Track closure

Authorized teams acknowledge and close reports.

Verification, shared reports, authority assignment, push notifications and AI detection are planned features—not active in this front-end demo.

05 / Trust & safety

Built around responsible reporting

01

Freshness matters

Reports should display when they were submitted and expire or be reviewed when stale.

02

Unverified means unverified

Never present a community report as a guaranteed fact or safety assurance.

03

Driver-safe alerts

Do not require users to interact with a screen while driving. Pull over before reporting.

04

Privacy by design

Ask permission for location and avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.
